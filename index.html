How to read this register

  • Everything here is what owners reported on the forum. Nothing has been tested by us. Check fitment with a specialist before buying.
  • "Not stated" in the part number column means the thread never gave the Lotus number. Those numbers are still to be found.
  • Several alternatives come from one owner's single post. Those are marked Unverified until someone else confirms them.
  • Rows marked No known alternative still name any workaround owners used, such as repairing the original or finding used stock.
